#### **Submission Instructions:**

Submission of papers will be handled electronically via **EDAS**.

- **PAPER SUBMISSION INSTRUCTION \[ONLY FOR REVIEW MANUSCRIPT\]**
  - Prospective authors are invited to submit manuscripts reporting **original unpublished research and recent developments** in the topics related to the conference. All submitted articles will be evaluated on their quality and relevance using **double-blind peer review**. All review manuscripts should fulfil the double-blind submission guidelines for review purposes. For further details please follow **[here](https://ieee-uemcon.org/call-for-papers/)**. When preparing your proposal, please take the following procedure as a minimum:
  - Eliminate the authors’ names and affiliations from the title page.
  - Receipts of identifying names and financial sources should not be there.
  - Papers should follow the [**IEEE preprint policy.**](https://cis.ieee.org/images/files/Publications/IEEE_Preprint_Policy.pdf)
  - UEMCON conducts a **plagiarism check** on all submitted papers. Plagiarism and self-plagiarism apply to all works.

#### **Paper Acceptance Criteria:**

  - ##### A paper should hold three majority acceptance without any strong reject for being accepted.
  - ##### A strong reject with proper justification will lead to rejection of the paper.
  - ##### An acceptable AI score on Turnitin is typically below 20%. Scores from 1% to 19% may include false positives. ([http://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/guidelines-for-generative-ai-usage](http://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/guidelines-for-generative-ai-usage))

##### **PAPER SUBMISSION INSTRUCTIONS \[FOR FINAL MANUSCRIPT\]**

- *Please comply with the following guidelines.*
  - It is required that the manuscript follows the standard camera-ready format (**standard format, double column, 10-point font**). Please double-check the paper size in your page setup to make sure you are using the letter-size paper layout (8.5 inch X 11 inch). The paper should not contain **page numbers or any special headers or footers.**
  - In order to achieve the same appearance for all manuscripts, UEMCON requires the usage of the templates in Microsoft Word (US Letter) and LaTeX format can be found at  **Manuscript Templates for Conference Proceedings.**
